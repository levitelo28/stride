Stride Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

In short: Stride uses your training and health data only to plan your training, show your progress and, if you turn it on, write your AI coach review. We never sell your data, never use it for advertising, and you can export or delete all of it from the app at any time.

Who we are

Stride is operated by Levi Teleshevsky (“we”, “us”). Questions or requests about your data: telachef@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, used to sign you in with a one-time link.
  • Training: your goal, training plan, workouts you log, and the effort ratings and notes you add.
  • Apple Health (iPhone, only if you connect it): workouts (type, date, start time, duration, distance, average and peak heart rate, and the app that recorded them), sleep, resting heart rate and heart-rate variability. iOS lets you choose exactly which of these Stride may read. Stride only reads from Apple Health; it never writes to it.
  • Web app (stridehealth.netlify.app): anything you enter there, such as meals and meal photos, check-ins, sleep and strength sessions, and workouts imported from a Strava export file.

We don’t collect your location, contacts or advertising identifiers, and Stride contains no advertising or third-party analytics.

How we use it

  • To build and adjust your training plan and show your training, recovery and progress.
  • To keep your data in sync between your devices.
  • For the AI coach, only if you turn it on (see below).

We do not use your data, including any data from Apple Health, for advertising, marketing or data mining, and we never sell it or share it with data brokers.

The AI coach

The coach is off until you turn it on. When it’s on and you ask for a review, Stride sends a summary of your last six weeks to Anthropic, the maker of Claude, to write it. The summary contains your goal, planned and completed sessions, workout distances, durations and heart rate, your effort ratings and notes, average sleep, and resting heart rate and HRV compared with your own baseline. It does not include your name or email address.

Anthropic processes the summary to produce your review and, under its commercial terms, does not use it to train its models. You can turn the coach off at any time in the Coach tab; nothing more is sent after that. Saved reviews stay in your account until you delete them or your account. The coach gives training guidance, not medical advice.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and stored in a database where access rules limit each account to its own data. Sign-in uses one-time email links, so there is no password to steal.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while you have an account. In the app, Settings → Privacy and data lets you:

  • Export my data: download everything in your account as a JSON file.
  • Delete my account: permanently delete your account and all its data, including photos and coach reviews. This happens immediately and can’t be undone. Data in Apple Health is not affected.

To stop Stride reading Apple Health, go to iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Stride. You can also email us to access, correct or delete your data.

Children

Stride is not intended for anyone under 16, and we don’t knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
